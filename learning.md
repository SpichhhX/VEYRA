Start at a comfortable listening level. If recording, select the correct input and leave enough headroom to avoid clipping.</p><h2>3. Make an eight-bar idea</h2><p>Choose a tempo. Use contrast: remove or add parts rather than filling every moment.</p><h2>5. Mix the essentials</h2><p>Balance track volumes first. Use panning and simple EQ adjustments where needed. Check your mix quietly as well as at a normal listening level.</p><h2>6. Export and listen</h2><p>Export using the DAW’s render or bounce command.
